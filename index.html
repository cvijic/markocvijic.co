What I do

Not a channel. The whole commercial system.

Companies rarely call me about a channel. They call because something at the business level stopped working: growth flattened while spend kept rising, a category is being lost in search and nobody can say when it started, a seven-figure ad budget has no owner who can defend it, a launch into a new market has to land the first time, or a name that carries the company is being defined by whatever a search engine decides to show.

Those look like separate problems. They are usually one. The commercial side of the business was assembled channel by channel, each with its own vendor, its own definition of a conversion and its own report, so no single view exists of how a euro becomes revenue. Fix a channel inside that and you get a better-looking dashboard, not a better business.

Seven capabilities, one system. Each has its own page, because each is a different problem with a different first move.

About

Why the center.

Every partner in the firm got a position on the floor. I am the center, number five. I hold the paint: the measurement, the attribution, the governance under everything else. Nobody applauds the center — a rebound never makes the highlight reel.

But no offense runs without someone holding the back of it down, and no play works if the paint is open. It is the same instinct in the work: I would rather own the unglamorous layer everyone assumes is already handled, and be the person in the room who can say what a number means and then defend it when the answer is inconvenient.

The longer version

Let us look at your numbers before anyone proposes anything.
