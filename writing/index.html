Recurring themes

The same five arguments.

Last-click is a decision, not a default

Every budget allocated on last-click data has silently defunded the channels that create demand in favour of the channel that closes it. Nobody chose that. The tool chose it, and the tool was never asked.

Analytics and attribution →
Share of voice needs five equations

Visibility, click share, category share, paid coverage and answer-engine presence are five different questions. Collapsing them into one figure produces a number that cannot be acted on and is usually wrong in a flattering direction.

Crawl budget is a budget

Large catalogs generate tens of thousands of near-duplicate URLs through their own filters, and search engines spend their finite attention there instead of on the pages that sell. It is invisible in every dashboard and obvious in the logs.

Ecommerce SEO →
AI traffic, at actual volume

Answer engines are genuinely changing how queries get intercepted, and the traffic they send is currently a fraction of what the discourse implies. Both halves of that sentence matter, and most commentary picks one.

Enterprise SEO →
Processes fail quietly

A broken conversion event, a bid strategy optimising toward nothing, a report nobody opens. None of these announce themselves. They just quietly change the outcome, which is why governance is the actual product.
